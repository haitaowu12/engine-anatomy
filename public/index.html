Read the boundaries, too.

Engine controls regulate engine response. Helicopter flight controls set rotor pitch; the chosen turboprop has a separate propeller speed governor; wings create lift in fixed-wing aircraft. None of these models flies the aircraft or represents a manufacturer’s control law.

Three architectures, with stated limits.

The shapes, numerical parameters, efficiencies, pressure splits and controller gains are synthetic. The models explain energy and control causality; they do not predict a calibrated engine or certified aircraft response.

Gas and shaft balances, actuator lag and the chosen governors are simulated. Airframe geometry and health/channel concepts are explanatory. Idealized thrust/hover-force estimates are labeled; no six-degree-of-freedom flight, surge, start, blade stress or fault-accommodation model is present.

Temperature probe locations are explicit. Calculated turbine-inlet T4 is never labeled as measured EGT/ITT. Optional bleed/variable-vane and redundancy interfaces are configuration dependent, not universal FADEC features.
