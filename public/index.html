BOUNDARIES THAT MATTER

The engine is part of an aircraft.

Shafts carry mechanical energy; dashed violet links in the scene carry information. Electrical and oil services are representative interfaces; their circuit dynamics are not simulated.

MECHANICAL

Power turbine → reduction → rotor

Rotor pitch changes aerodynamic load. Load torque acts back on the power shaft. The governor changes fuel to recover speed.

ELECTRICAL & SERVICE

Accessory drive → pumps & generator

A running electrical supply powers sensing and control; oil supports bearings and cooling. The model includes a fixed accessory power draw.

INFORMATION

Sensors → control → cockpit

Speed feedback closes the governor loop. Ng, Np, temperature and output power inform the display below. Real sensing, redundancy and limits vary.

SAME PRINCIPLES, DIFFERENT JOB

And the turbofan?

Both use a compressor, combustor and turbines. In a typical two-spool turbofan, the low-pressure turbine drives the fan on one shaft and the high-pressure turbine drives the core compressor on another. Bypass air contributes thrust.

Here, the free turbine instead delivers shaft power to an external load. This release simulates only the turboshaft.

WHAT THIS MODEL CAN TELL YOU

A transparent teaching model.

Understand energy transfer, independent shaft motion and closed-loop response. Every numerical value is a synthetic assumption, not a published engine specification.

What runs

Mass continuity (gas = air + fuel); constant-cp heat/work balances; two shaft torque balances with inertia; a lagged fuel actuator; a PI speed governor with anti-windup and illustrative fuel, Ng and temperature targets. Pressure ratio follows a simple speed relation instead of calibrated component maps.

What does not run

Start/stop, surge or stall, combustion stability, blade stresses, emissions, flight performance, sensor faults, real FADEC redundancy, oil circuits and electrical bus dynamics. All are outside this model’s validation scope. No engine-specific control logic or performance maps are included.
